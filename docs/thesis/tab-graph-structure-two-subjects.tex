\begin{table}[htbp]
  \centering
  \caption{Resulting graph structure for the two pilot subjects under an identical extraction
    procedure (\texttt{openai/gpt-oss-120b}, \texttt{graph.gleanings}~$=0$, same prompt and entity

    opposite shapes: passc yields 69\% more entities per chunk yet connects them far less, with
    two thirds of apd's relationships per entity, a mean degree of 2.25 against 3.39, and a
    quarter of its entities isolated. This is the signature of conceptual slide prose, which names
    many ideas per slide and asserts few relations between them, against apd's code and algorithm
    listings where a chunk names a tightly coupled cluster. Local search traverses relationships,
